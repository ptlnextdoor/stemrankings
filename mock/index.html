How it works

  1. Claim it. You get 14 days. Up to 3 students can work on the same package.
  2. Work in your own notebook or repo. The reviewer answers questions about the paper but does not write your code.
  3. Submit a link and a 200-word summary. The reviewer checks method, data, and that the numbers reproduce.
  4. Passed work is sent to the author as a memo, and stays on your record.
