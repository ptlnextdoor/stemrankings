Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

needalab (needalab.com) helps students find research labs and write emails to professors. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions or requests: hello@needalab.com.

Who can use needalab

You must be 13 or older to create an account. We ask for your birth year and block accounts under 13.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your name, and your birth year. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We don't receive your Google password.
  • What you give us: resume, bio or project text you paste or upload; the professors you save; notes, statuses and professor email addresses you enter; and the email drafts we write for you.
  • Purchases: Stripe processes payments. We get your plan status and a Stripe customer ID. We never see or store your card number.
  • Usage limits: to prevent abuse we count searches per account, or per approximate network address if you're signed out. We store a one-way hash, not the address itself.

We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell your data.

How we use it

  • To find professors, build your profile, and draft emails you asked for.
  • To run your account, credits and plan.
  • To contact you about your account or ask for feedback. You can ask us to stop.

Services that process your data

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini): to write your profile and email drafts. We send your profile text and the professor's public papers. Their API terms say this data isn't used to train their models.
  • OpenAlex, ORCID, Crossref, Europe PMC, Serper: public research data and search, used to find professors and their public contact pages. We send professor names, not your personal data.
  • Stripe: payments. Resend: sign-in emails. GitHub Pages / Cloudflare: hosting and DNS.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use your Google name and email only to create and identify your account. If we later offer "Send from Gmail", it will ask separately for permission to send email, and we will use that permission only to send emails you explicitly choose to send, from your own account, to the professor you selected. We won't read your inbox.

needalab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use Google user data for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't let humans read it except with your consent, for security, or as required by law, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete documents and saved professors any time in the app. To download a copy of everything we store, or delete your whole account and everything in it, open My profile > Your data. Deleting also cancels any plan. If you can't sign in, email hello@needalab.com from your account's address and we'll do it within 30 days.

Security

Each account can only read its own data (database row-level security). Payments and credits are handled only on our servers. No system is perfectly secure; tell us if you find a problem.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell signed-in users.
